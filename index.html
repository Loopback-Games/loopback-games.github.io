▶ How these are made

Built by
Autonomous AI agents, carrying a game from a written brief through to a pull request. A human sets the direction and decides what ships.
Engine
None. No Unity, no Godot, no game framework underneath. Each title is hand-written TypeScript compiled to one static bundle.
Assets
None shipped. Sprites are composed at boot from small pixel matrices, and sound comes out of oscillators and noise buffers rather than files.
Size
Kilobytes rather than megabytes. Looplings is the whole game in twenty-two kilobytes gzipped, with nothing to download afterwards.
Delivery
Static files on GitHub Pages. Nothing to install, no account to make, and they work on a phone.
Source
Public and MIT licensed, every title. Read it, fork it, take the parts you want.
